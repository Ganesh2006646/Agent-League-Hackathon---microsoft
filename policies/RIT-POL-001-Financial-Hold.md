# RIT-POL-001: Student Financial Hold and Service Restriction Policy
**Redmond Institute of Technology (RIT) — Office of the Bursar & Student Finance**

## §1. Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the conditions under which financial holds are placed on student accounts due to outstanding financial obligations to Redmond Institute of Technology (RIT), the specific services restricted by these holds, and the mechanisms for hold resolution. This policy applies to all registered students (undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional).

## §2. Student Financial Responsibility Agreement
Upon registration for any academic term, every student must electronically sign the **RIT Student Financial Responsibility Agreement**. By signing, the student accepts full legal and financial responsibility for all tuition, mandatory fees, housing charges, meal plans, and related costs.

## §3. Payment Deadlines and Grace Periods
3.1. All tuition and fee payments are due by 5:00 PM PT on the first day of instruction for the respective academic term.
3.2. A standard **14-day grace period** is automatically granted starting from the payment deadline. During this grace period, no late fees or holds are applied.
3.3. Balances remaining unpaid after the 14-day grace period will incur a monthly late fee of **1.0% of the outstanding balance** (up to a maximum of $150 per month) until the account is settled.

## §4. Placement of Financial Holds (Bursar Holds)
4.1. An administrative **Financial Hold** (often referred to as a "Bursar Hold") will be automatically placed on any student account with an outstanding balance **greater than $500.00 USD** that remains unpaid after the 14-day grace period.
4.2. Accounts with outstanding balances of $500.00 USD or less will incur late fees but will **not** trigger a Financial Hold, ensuring that minor fees (such as library or parking fines) do not disrupt course access.

## §5. Services Restricted Under Financial Hold
Once a Financial Hold is active, the following RIT services are immediately restricted:
- **Course Enrollment**: Inability to register for subsequent academic terms, or to add/drop courses in the current term.
- **IT and Learning Access**: Suspension of the student's RIT M365 account, restricting access to:
  - RIT Institutional Email (Outlook)
  - Learning Management System (LMS - Canvas)
  - Campus Wi-Fi Networks
- **Library and Lab Access**: Suspension of physical entry to university libraries, computer laboratories, and specialized research facilities.
- **Academic Credentials**: Withholding of official transcripts, enrollment verifications, and physical diplomas.

## §6. Hold Removal and System Reactivation
6.1. A Financial Hold will be cleared automatically by the system once the outstanding balance is paid in full, bringing the account balance below the $500.00 USD threshold.
6.2. Following payment clearance, system synchronization occurs on a **24-hour cycle**. Once cleared in the Finance Ledger, the account state will update to `accountEnabled: true` within M365 and LMS within this window.
6.3. Students may request **Immediate Manual Reactivation** through the RIT Front Desk if they present a valid bank-cleared receipt showing full payment.

## §7. Partial Payment and Course Access Restructuring
7.1. In accordance with RIT's commitment to academic continuity, students who clear **80% or more of their total outstanding term balance** may request a **Restructured Course Access** provision.
7.2. Upon manual verification by the Financial Aid office, the student's account will be reactivated, but restricted to **core course access only** (no elective course access on LMS, and library physical services remain suspended).
7.3. Full service access will only be restored once the remaining 20% balance is cleared.

## §8. Escalation and Collection Timelines
- **Day 15 (Post-Deadline)**: Placement of Financial Hold; email and Teams notifications sent to student.
- **Day 30**: Enrollment cancellation warning; academic advisors notified.
- **Day 60**: Mandatory enrollment cancellation and suspension of current term classes.
- **Day 90**: Referral of delinquent account to external collection agencies; reporting to national credit bureaus.
