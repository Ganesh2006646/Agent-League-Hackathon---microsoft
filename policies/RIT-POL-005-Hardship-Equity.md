# RIT-POL-005: Student Financial Hardship and Equity Policy
**Redmond Institute of Technology (RIT) — Office of Student Equity & Support**

## §1. Policy Philosophy
Redmond Institute of Technology (RIT) recognizes that sudden financial hardships (family emergencies, natural disasters, banking delays, or socioeconomic barriers) should not immediately disrupt a student’s academic progress. This policy establishes safety nets to ensure equitable access to learning resources.

## §2. Emergency 72-Hour Access Extension
2.1. Students who do not meet the 80% partial payment threshold (RIT-POL-001 §7) but have filed a **Financial Hardship Application** may request a temporary emergency access extension.
2.2. Upon confirmation of the hardship application filing, the system is authorized to reactivate the student's IT and Canvas LMS accounts for a period of **72 hours** (3 business days).
2.3. Emergency access will automatically expire at 11:59 PM PT on the third business day unless a formal payment plan is finalized or a waiver is approved by the Bursar.
2.4. Only **one** Emergency Access Extension may be granted per student per academic term.

## §3. Integration with Financial Aid and Scholarships
When a hardship extension is requested, the system must query the Financial Aid Ledger. If there is a pending, un-disbursed scholarship or grant that covers the outstanding balance, the agent is authorized to bypass standard holds and approve **Full Access Reactivation** under a "Pending Aid" status.

## §4. Accessibility and Digital Inclusion Requirements
4.1. In compliance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, all account status alerts, emails, and portal notifications must be compatible with assistive technologies (screen readers, high-contrast displays, text-to-speech).
4.2. Notifications must avoid jargon, offer clear instructions for next steps, and provide direct links to support offices.
4.3. Front Desk agents may request voice-prompt verification or telephone callbacks for students with visual or motor impairments who face difficulty navigating digital verification channels.
